REAL-TIME YOUTUBE ACCESSIBILITY

🤟 YTSign

A live sign-language overlay that automatically follows the video.

  1. Open a YouTube video.
  2. Choose your settings and press Enable on YouTube.
  3. The best available video text or speech source is selected automatically.
English, Urdu, and Hindi video text is sent directly for pose translation. When text is unavailable, multilingual speech recognition runs locally. The 3D signer prioritizes clear, reliable motion.
Prototype output may contain linguistic or motion errors. Not a replacement for a qualified interpreter.
