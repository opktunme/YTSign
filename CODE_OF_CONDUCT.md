# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, experiences, and communication needs.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people using the project.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention, intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
