# PSL review worksheet

Use this worksheet with a fluent PSL signer, preferably a Deaf PSL user, to review the avatar at its normal overlay size. Mechanical tests show whether controls move; they do not establish whether the signing is correct. Generated poses, gloss labels, and service responses are candidate outputs, not ground truth. Judge them against the source meaning and the reviewer's PSL knowledge, allowing legitimate regional variants.

## Review each short clip

1. Select **Pakistan (PSL)**. Record the video link, source language, video timestamp range, extension version, and overlay size.
2. Watch the avatar at normal playback speed. Before reading the source phrase, record what the signing appears to mean and anything unclear.
3. Compare that interpretation with the spoken phrase and its context. Verify the transcript itself; for audio fallback, separately check the recognized English phrase shown by YTSign. Record transcription mistakes separately from signing mistakes.
4. Replay slowly to locate errors, then watch again at normal speed. A handshape that is readable only when paused does not pass normal-speed intelligibility.

## Checklist

- [ ] **Meaning:** The output conveys the intended phrase, including names, numbers, negation, questions, tense/time, and relevant context. No added or omitted meaning.
- [ ] **Handshape:** Both hands have five visible digits where the pose permits. Each finger and thumb bends at the intended joints; check base, middle, and tip bends, spread, thumb opposition, and fingertip contact. No frozen digits, impossible bends, skin tears, or merged fingers that obscure the sign.
- [ ] **Palm orientation and location:** Palm/finger direction and placement relative to the body, face, and other hand are correct. Do not accept a camera-facing palm just because it is easier to see.
- [ ] **Movement:** Path, direction, repetition, speed, holds, and transitions preserve the intended sign. No unintended lexical gesture appears while moving between signs.
- [ ] **Two-handed coordination:** Dominant and supporting hands retain their roles; timing, symmetry/asymmetry, crossings, and contacts are appropriate.
- [ ] **Face and other nonmanual cues:** Check expression, eyebrows, gaze, mouth, head, and body cues needed for the phrase. **Known limitation:** the current textured-avatar renderer does not drive facial grammar or mouth movements. Mark a phrase as rejected when this omission loses or changes required meaning.
- [ ] **PSL grammar and intelligibility:** The sequence is natural and understandable PSL, with appropriate spatial reference and phrase structure; it is not merely a sequence of signs following spoken-word order.
- [ ] **Overlay readability and timing:** Face and signing hands stay visible at the normal chosen size. Hands do not disappear behind the torso or leave the frame. Delay or queueing does not make it unclear which part of the video is being interpreted.

Record accepted clips as well as rejected ones. Prioritize meaning, finger articulation, orientation, and clear signing over minor hair or clothing imperfections. Recheck each correction at normal speed and retain the earlier record. Acceptance applies only to the reviewed clip and conditions; it is not certification of PSL output generally.
